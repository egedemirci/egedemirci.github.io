description: My research focuses on geometric machine learning, with an emphasis on symmetry, equivariance, and representation learning. I am interested in how symmetry and curvature shape what graph and language models learn, and in using that structure to build more data-efficient and interpretable representations. Most recently, I developed Concorde, a mixed-curvature framework for link prediction (NeurIPS 2026), and FlowSymm, a symmetry-preserving graph attention mechanism for network flow completion (ICLR 2026). I have also worked on calibrated equivariance, how language models represent cyclic concepts, and the graph reasoning capabilities of generative models.
